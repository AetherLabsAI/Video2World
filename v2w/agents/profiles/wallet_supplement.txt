Wallet task supplement (rigid and elastic submissions are both supported):
Rigid: protocol.json state_kind="rigid_object_pose"; submit a dynamic triangle
mesh named target, omit target.deformable. The evaluator creates a dynamic rigid
body with a convex-hull collider and observes the full submitted surface.
Elastic: state_kind="complete_deformable_surface"; submit a closed, consistently
oriented triangular shell and target.deformable.model="closed_particle_shell".
Example elastic parameters: particle_radius_m=0.001, stretch_stiffness=1000,
bend_stiffness=1, shear_stiffness=500, damping=0.1, pressure=0.
Both modes use metres/kg/seconds and at most 5000 vertices / 20000 triangles.
Author only initial object state; robot joint drives produce later motion.
No pinning, object attachment or post-start pose/shape injection is accepted.

For insertion, submit a physically open receiver mesh with side walls and bottom.
receiver.interior uses entity_local center, half_extents_xy, bottom_z and rim_z;
its cavity metadata must agree with the physical mesh. The object center must
pass through the mouth, release and remain stable inside for the final 1 second.
Protrusion above the rim is allowed. Complete surface containment is not required.
Pick stages are grasp, lift at least 3cm, and terminal holding at least 5cm high;
use the mesh bottom, not an arbitrary model origin. Public task_rules gives times.
Both rigid and elastic wallet candidates use fingertip proximity and sustained
relative holding as evidence; no contact forces are invented.

Validate with staged wallet_contract.py. Actions must have exactly
public_task.control_frames rows at 20Hz, followed by a fixed evaluator-owned
2-second settle window. Do not lengthen actions to add attempts.
